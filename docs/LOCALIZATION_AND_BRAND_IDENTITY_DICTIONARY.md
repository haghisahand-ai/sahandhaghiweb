## 6. Persian Typographic Standards & Half-Space (`نیم‌فاصله`) Policy

To prevent disjointed or machine-like Persian rendering, all Persian strings strictly adhere to the following rules:

1. **Mandatory Half-Space (ZWNJ — `\u200c` / `&zwnj;`):**
   - Plural markers: `پروژه‌ها` (NOT `پروژه ها` or `پروژها`)
   - Compound nouns: `نرم‌افزار` (NOT `نرم افزار`), `راه‌حل‌ها` (NOT `راه حل ها`), `هوش‌مصنوعی` or `هوش مصنوعی`
   - Prefixes/Suffixes: `می‌شود` (NOT `میشود`), `توسعه‌دهنده` (NOT `توسعه دهنده`), `دیدگاه‌ها` (NOT `دیدگاه ها`)
2. **Proper Punctuation:**
   - Arabic/Persian comma: `،` (NOT English `,`)
   - Arabic/Persian semicolon: `؛` (NOT English `;`)
   - Arabic/Persian question mark: `؟` (NOT English `?`)
3. **Persian Directional Flow:**
   - Text elements wrap in `dir="rtl"`.
   - Technical strings, URLs, code blocks, and math formulas retain isolated `dir="ltr"` and `font-family: var(--font-mono)`.
